## Scope

1. Capture bounded stdout/stderr and native exit information separately without turning known harmless warnings into random PowerShell exceptions.
2. Classify missing codec, damaged input, denied access, resource exhaustion and recoverable failures.
3. Retry only justified transient failures with a bound; preserve promised alpha/background treatment on every attempt.

- A missing codec is not retried at every scale.
- No fallback silently removes required transparency handling or accepts nonzero output as ordinary success.
